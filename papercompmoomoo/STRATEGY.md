# Active paper strategies

Both strategies trade only the configured US Moomoo `SIMULATE` `COMPETITION` account. The account is shared for buying power and duplicate-symbol control; candidate, position, trade, and evidence ledgers remain strategy-specific. All market timestamps use `America/New_York` (ET), and OpenD market state must confirm an order session.

Gainer may add to an existing symbol position when a new candidate passes the live entry gates. An existing holding does not reject the candidate or consume a second unique-symbol slot; a working order for that symbol still blocks a duplicate submission. Every add-on still passes current buying-power, concentration, per-trade risk, tape, and account-cap checks. Moomoo's updated aggregate quantity and average cost are reconciled after the fill, while existing exit lines and high-water marks continue to ratchet only upward. The immutable journal records gate-by-gate evidence, later reevaluations, order outcome, and add-on fill reconciliation.

## Gainers

`src/papercompmoomoo/bot.py` polls Moomoo's US hottest-gainers list, filters to Nasdaq-listed names, and uses Moomoo quotes, mover volume pressure, candidate-specific native one-minute bars, and price/CLV Kalman inflection evidence. Entry also waits for at least five current-session candles, with at least 20% carrying real traded volume (minimum two) and a trade within five minutes; zero-volume carried prices remain chart evidence but do not count as an active tape. This is a rolling gate, so a candidate becomes eligible again when its own tape activity improves. From 04:00 ET, it may submit `SIMULATE` limit buys with explicit `Session.ETH` only when OpenD confirms a supported premarket state. During RTH it uses explicit `Session.RTH`; the 15:00 ET cutoff blocks new entries. It does not fetch broad-universe candles or use legacy non-Moomoo bars for live sizing decisions.

## ORB

`tools/orb_premarket.py` consumes Gainers' fresh Moomoo movers snapshot, filters and ranks Nasdaq candidates, and fetches candles only for selected candidates. `tools/orb_paper.py` prepares premarket evidence but enters only during the RTH opening window after OpenD confirms regular trading and the opening-range, VWAP, volume-pressure, and confirmation gates pass. It requires active traded volume in the recent tape and at least three traded candles in the five-bar opening range; flat zero-volume bars cannot form an ORB entry. Failed tape checks are reevaluated on later scans, so a symbol can qualify once trading resumes. ORB entries use explicit `Session.RTH`; no premarket breakout order is sent before the regular-session opening range exists.

## Exits and evidence

The exit worker manages open positions in both strategies throughout the supported 04:00–20:00 ET weekday window. The ratcheting protective stop, full target, 70% line, intermediate line, midpoint, and audited `thomasai_liquidity_trap_exit` are deterministic triggers; no discretionary model reviews or unrelated time exits replace them. Every exit line ratchets upward only. The 70% line tracks 70% of the current full-target distance and is persisted with a max-against-previous rule. RTH exits use market orders. ETH exits use a limit at the current executable bid and are repriced to the latest fresh bid. If neither provider-time quote nor book data is fresh, the trigger stays latched and no stale-priced order is submitted. If an ETH exit remains at the RTH open, it is cancelled and replaced with an RTH market order. At 15:00 ET the worker flattens all open positions, one hour before the normal 16:00 ET close. Preserve every trigger, order, cancellation, and broker-confirmed fill as immutable evidence; order acceptance is not a fill.

The 100% target exits as soon as a fresh Moomoo traded price touches or crosses above the line, with the confirmed trigger latched while a fresh executable exit quote is obtained. Every other price line exits on a confirmed downward cross: both fresh Moomoo traded price and executable bid must be below the line on two distinct quote timestamps at least 500 ms apart, with the second traded price no higher than the first. A rebound above a downward-exit line resets confirmation. This avoids treating a low book bid as a traded-price cross and does not wait for a one-minute candle close. Confirmation evidence is recorded with the exit decision. Line values and ratchet rules are unchanged. Levels and realized P&L use the Gainer position's persisted original entry; Moomoo's remaining-lot `cost_price` can shift after partial sells and must not replace that entry. Gainer stop, trail, and target terms use that symbol's prior Moomoo candles and, when needed, its current candidate candles. ORB trail width uses that symbol's Moomoo candles at entry and updates from its own post-entry candles. Live spread and minimum tick size set a symbol-specific minimum bracket width. Generic percentage defaults are not accepted as calibrated terms: candidates wait for enough valid symbol candles, while an already-open Gainer position without usable risk evidence falls back to its persisted stop or an entry-price safety floor.

## Dashboard and session readiness

The portal Overview shows shared Competition account equity, P&amp;L, cash, and buying power, followed by current or most recent session charts for Gainers and ORB. The separate Gainer Tracker tab shows current-session Nasdaq detections and Gainer approval/rejection outcomes. Every account chart names its strategy and Competition SIMULATE account; candidate charts can show candidates that have not traded. Prior-session symbols must not be mixed into the current-session view.

Before premarket, verify OpenD connectivity, account type, market state, running Gainers and exit-worker processes, Moomoo mover freshness, candidate-scoped subscriptions, and writable ledgers. A refresh request alone does not establish freshness: use provider timestamps, and leave a mover waiting when Moomoo returns an old quote or incomplete book. Before RTH, verify ORB candidates and the opening-range process. On every restart reconcile positions, working orders, and fills before permitting new entries. See [`../docs/MOOMOO.md`](../docs/MOOMOO.md) for the detailed session runbook.

## Research replay

`tools/research_replay.py` and `research_replay_worker.py` replay only symbols actually filled in the current New York session. The worker waits until 20:05 ET, after extended hours close, checks Moomoo history quota, and backfills only traded symbols with candle gaps. It uses Moomoo-native OHLCV candles, recorded entry terms, the shared ratcheting 100%, 70%, intermediate, midpoint, and red-stop rules, and the 15:00 ET position flatten. Every 15 minutes it evaluates 10,000 randomized exit-grid configurations against today's stored candles and records the per-symbol top ten. These are in-sample research results and do not change live settings. The replay tab shows the latest session at `/exit-replay`. Each run is retained in `data/research_replay.sqlite3` and the latest report is written to `data/strategy_research.json`. OHLC bars cannot reconstruct exact intrabar trigger order or market-order fills; unresolved candle gaps or levels stay visible and excluded from P&amp;L.

Replay is offline-only. The entry point refuses to run on weekdays from 04:00 through 20:00 ET, so it cannot compete with premarket, regular, or extended-hours trading. Run it after 20:00 ET, once extended trading is closed, or on weekends. Today's trades enter replay scope only after 20:00 ET.
