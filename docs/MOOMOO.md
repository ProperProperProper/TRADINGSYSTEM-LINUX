# Moomoo live-session operating contract

This is the operating reference for the two active paper strategies. Both use Moomoo OpenD and the configured US `SIMULATE` `COMPETITION` account. OpenD listens locally at `127.0.0.1:11111`; the Python SDK version is pinned in `versions.lock`. Orders are allowed only after the code verifies the configured account is present, is a US Competition simulated account, and the live market state supports the requested order session.

## Session schedule and responsibilities

All session times use `America/New_York` (ET), including DST changes and holidays. Wall-clock time alone never authorizes an order: the relevant OpenD US market state must agree.

| ET window | Gainers | ORB | Exit worker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Before 04:00 | No new entries. | No new entries. | No order submission. |
| 04:00–09:30 premarket | Poll Moomoo US hottest gainers, keep Nasdaq-listed candidates current, fetch candidate-only quotes/bars, run the entry gates, and submit `SIMULATE` `ETH` limit buys when OpenD reports a supported premarket state. | Consume the shared Moomoo movers snapshot, rank/filter candidates, and prepare candidate-specific evidence. No ORB entry before RTH. | Subscribe to open holdings; evaluate executable quotes and all ratcheting exit lines. Triggered exits use `ETH` marketable limit sells at the current executable bid. |
| 09:30–15:00 regular session | Continue fresh candidate discovery and gated entries using `RTH`; do not open a position after the 15:00 cutoff. | Evaluate the opening range, VWAP, volume pressure, and confirmation conditions; submit only after OpenD confirms `MORNING`/RTH. | Price-line, liquidity-trap, or flatten signals exit through the verified Competition paper account. RTH exits use market orders. Leftover ETH exits are cancelled and replaced by RTH market orders. |
| 15:00–16:00 regular session | No new entries. | No new entries. | Flatten every open strategy position at 15:00 ET (one hour before the normal 16:00 close); continue reconciling broker fills. |
| 16:00–20:00 extended session | No new entries. | No new entries. | Continue managing carried positions and triggered exits with `ETH` marketable limit sells when OpenD confirms an extended-hours state. |
| After 20:00 | No new entries. | No new entries. | No order submission until the next supported session. Any unfilled exit remains visible and must be reconciled before new-session trading. |

The configured Competition account has broker-recorded US orders with `session=ETH` and `FILLED_ALL` status. This account-specific broker history supports the current ETH workflow; it is stronger evidence for this account than generic API guidance. Moomoo's generic [paper-trading Q&amp;A](https://openapi.moomoo.com/moomoo-api-doc/en/qa/trade.html) describes ETH support more narrowly. The worker verifies account type and market state on each run, and all fill claims must come from broker order/deal records. An accepted or modified order is not a fill.

## Candidate data and API load

Gainers is the first mover-discovery source. It queries Moomoo's US hottest-gainers list every 10 seconds and filters for Nasdaq-listed securities; this uses at most three calls per 30 seconds against the documented 60-call limit. ORB consumes the short-lived local snapshot instead of issuing a competing broad-market scan. Quotes and native one-minute candles are fetched only for identified candidates and open positions, and are shared locally when both strategies need the same symbol. Do not constrain discovery to SPY holdings or fetch broad-universe candle history. Release subscriptions for symbols that are neither current candidates nor open positions.

All `RecordedContext` trade clients share a persistent SQLite rolling-window limiter for `order_list_query`: at most eight calls in any 30 seconds, leaving two calls below Moomoo's observed 10-call ceiling for other local clients. This covers Gainer, ORB, and the exit worker together; calls wait for a slot rather than being sent over the cap. Fill reconciliation is throttled, and exit-order maintenance skips empty accounts.

Premarket evidence must be current for the New York session date. Preserve provider timestamps and distinguish premarket (04:00–09:30), regular (09:30–16:00), and extended (16:00–20:00) observations. For the top 12 identified Nasdaq movers, subscribe to Moomoo quote and order-book pushes. If the bulk snapshot's provider timestamp is missing, future, or older than 30 seconds, actively request a batched `get_stock_quote` refresh and a rate-limited per-symbol `get_order_book` refresh (at most once per symbol every 30 seconds); use only provider timestamps, never local receipt time, to establish freshness. Keep the candidate waiting while either the quote or executable book remains stale. The bot may continue capturing Moomoo candles for already identified movers, but candle capture does not make stale quote/book evidence eligible for entry. Symbols outside the bounded live subscription set wait for a fresh bulk snapshot. For triggered ETH exits, a provider timestamp older than 30 seconds is rejected; the worker requests a fresh Moomoo market snapshot when an order-book read is stale. If neither source is fresh, it keeps the trigger latched and does not price a new exit from a stale quote. A stale quote, missing market state, unavailable account snapshot, or incomplete position/order reconciliation blocks a new entry rather than being treated as a pass.

## Entry and exit rules

- Gainers may enter during premarket and regular hours, subject to the Nasdaq, price, liquidity, volume-pressure, CLV/Kalman-inflection, account-capital, and fresh-quote gates. The maximum extension gate is 60% from the calculated base. Use a bounded marketable limit in `ETH`; use a market order only after Moomoo confirms `RTH`. Moomoo documents US market orders as RTH-only and recommends `Session` for pre/post-market orders.
- Do not leave entry orders parked: after 30 seconds, reprice a stale Gainer ETH limit from a fresh Moomoo ask, capped at the configured 0.5% limit adjustment, at most twice; then cancel it. RTH market orders are also cancelled if still unfilled after the same timeout. Record each reprice/cancel as immutable telemetry. Never reprice from a stale or missing quote.
- ORB uses premarket to discover and prepare candidates. It cannot form an opening-range breakout before the regular session; entries are permitted only during the configured RTH opening window after OpenD confirms the regular session and the opening-range/VWAP/volume/confirmation gates pass.
- Every order path must use the verified US Competition `SIMULATE` account. Shared buying power and duplicate-symbol ownership are coordinated through the atomic allocator; each strategy retains its own candidate, position, and trade ledger.
- The exit worker applies upward-ratcheting stop, 100% target, 70% target, intermediate, and midpoint lines, the audited `thomasai_liquidity_trap_exit`, and the mandatory 15:00 ET flatten. The 100% target sells on a fresh-price touch/up-cross; all other lines sell on a confirmed down-cross. A 100% touch is latched until the paper position closes. No discretionary model review or position-age exit replaces these rules. RTH triggers submit simulated market orders; ETH triggers use a limit at the live executable bid and reprice using fresh quotes. If the provider quote is older than 30 seconds, the worker requests a Moomoo snapshot and does not submit a stale-priced limit if no fresh executable quote is available. At RTH open, a remaining ETH exit is reconciled, cancelled if still working, and replaced with an RTH market exit. Preserve the original trigger and all order/fill events as immutable evidence.
- **Exit-line rule:** every line is an upward-only ratchet for the life of that position: red stop, midpoint, intermediate, green 70%, and 100% target. A recalculation may raise a line but must never lower it. The 100% target sells on an upward touch/cross; red stop, midpoint, intermediate, and 70% sell on confirmed downward crosses. The 70% line tracks 70% of the current full-target distance from entry and is independently maxed against its persisted value. This is a hard invariant for both bots and must be preserved in any exit or chart change.

## Dashboard and session readiness

The portal Overview shows shared Competition account equity, P&amp;L, cash, buying power, then current-session charts (or the most recent session when no current session has started) for Gainers and ORB. The separate Gainer Tracker tab shows current-session Nasdaq mover detections and Gainer approval/rejection outcomes. Gainer discovery evaluates every Nasdaq row in Moomoo's returned top-200 US movers list. Each account chart identifies its strategy and `COMPETITION SIMULATE` account. Symbol charts currently plot through 16:00 ET; they do not show 16:00–20:00 extended-hours candles. Candidate charts may include candidates that have not traded; symbols from unrelated sessions must not leak into the current-session view. Live quote updates must retain both provider time and the ET candle interval.

Open-position price lines come from the exit worker's `data/exit_levels.json` snapshot, not from chart estimates. The worker requires a broker position, strategy ownership, and a recoverable local entry timestamp (`opened_ms`) before it publishes levels. If a broker-held position is missing from this snapshot or lacks any valid line, treat that as an exit-management fault: reconcile its entry identity and broker/local state immediately. A chart showing `HOLDING` without stop/target lines is not evidence that the position is protected.

Before premarket, confirm OpenD connectivity, market-state reporting, both bot processes, the exit worker, configured Competition SIM account identity, fresh mover snapshot, candidate-scoped data feed, and writable audit ledgers. Confirm that every broker-held strategy position has a matching exit-level snapshot with stop and target lines. Before RTH, confirm the ORB candidate set and opening-range process are ready. After startup or restart, reconcile account positions, working orders, and fills before permitting entries. Service health is not proof that trading is ready; verify timestamped runtime status and broker records.

Keep OpenD credentials and the local account-ID map out of Git. Never publish the actual account number. See [`STRATEGY.md`](../papercompmoomoo/STRATEGY.md), [`SYSTEM.md`](SYSTEM.md), and [`EXIT_WORKER.md`](EXIT_WORKER.md) for code ownership and the learning/live-exit boundary.
