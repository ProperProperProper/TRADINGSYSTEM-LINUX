# Qwen learning status

Reviewed: 2026-11-06.

Qwen learning is shelved indefinitely. Its training MCP, trainer, scheduled review jobs, and learning-corpus refresh automation have been removed. Historical model, dataset, adapter, and immutable decision evidence remain on disk for reference. The local inference service is separate and remains installed for the cognitive brain and ThomasAI; Qwen has no live entry or exit authority.

The offline research replay is `papercompmoomoo/tools/research_replay.py`. It analyzes recorded sessions and traded symbols using the bots' own entry levels and Moomoo minute bars. It is blocked during weekday 04:00–20:00 ET and may run only after the extended session closes or on weekends. OHLC data cannot reconstruct historical executable bids or exact fills.
